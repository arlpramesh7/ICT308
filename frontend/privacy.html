Privacy notice

SmartDine is an educational restaurant discovery system. The initial restaurant catalogue and food photographs are illustrative, not representations of live partner businesses. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Information we use

Your account stores your name, email, password hash and role. We store your chosen cuisine, dietary preference, budget and search radius. Recommendations, offer notifications and ratings are linked to your account.

Location is your choice

We request browser location only when you select Use my location. You can choose a search area without sharing GPS. Coordinates are processed for that request and are not saved as a location history. Recommendation records can still imply that you searched near a venue.

External services

Walking directions open Google Maps. Selecting a directions link sends the chosen origin and restaurant coordinates to Google. Optional browser alerts send encrypted notifications through your browser provider, such as Google, Mozilla or Apple. Alerts depend on permission, network access and provider availability. In-app offers remain available without browser permission.

Your controls

From your account you can turn off promotional offers, export your data, clear recommendation and offer history, or delete your account. Account deletion removes your linked preferences, ratings and activity. Staff changes retain an audit record without your account identifier after deletion.

Protection and limits

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Sessions expire after one hour. Access is restricted by role and restaurant assignment. The local database is not encrypted; use trusted devices and avoid sensitive information. Network deployment requires HTTPS and protected storage. Backup deletion must be managed separately by the operator.

Dietary information and ranking

Vegetarian and vegan flags are venue supplied, not allergy guarantees. Confirm suitability directly. Rankings combine proximity, cuisine, dietary suitability, budget, ratings and a disclosed promotion contribution of up to five points. A score is a match indicator, not a guarantee of food quality.
